# [[Price vs Value (Mr Market)]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · why research is worth doing at all · sits before [[Equity Research Process (Story to Line Item)]]

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Objective:** price = what the market *feels* today; value = the cash the business produces ➔ short run a voting machine, long run a weighing machine.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** research only has an edge on the **long-term / fundamental** side — short-term price moves are largely unforecastable.

## 📝 Core
### 1. What you can own (investable instruments, in slide order)
- **Bank savings / fixed deposits** ➔ **mutual funds** (balanced, mixed-asset) ➔ **ETFs** (track broad market indices) ➔ **fixed income** (bonds, notes) ➔ **equity** (ownership in companies).
- **Private vs public equity** ➔ private = hedge funds, private equity, venture capital; public = the stock market.

### 2. Voting machine vs weighing machine
| | Short term — Technical Analysis | Long term — Fundamental Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Price set by | whoever is most eager to trade today | the cash the business produces (price converges on it) |
| Driven by | news flow, sentiment, flows, fear & greed | earnings power, returns on capital, reinvestment |
| Link to business | can stay disconnected **for years** | fundamentals dominate ➔ forecastable |
| Research edge | none — "do not build a career on it" | **the only place research has an edge** |

- **Mr Market** ➔ quotes a price every day; it tells you what he *feels*, not what the business is worth ➔ free to ignore him, free to exploit him.
- **Businesslike investing** ➔ "investing is most intelligent when it is most businesslike" ➔ analyse the stock as a stake in a business, not a ticker.

## 🧠 Active Recall
> [!FAQ]- A stock falls 20% on a gloomy news week while nothing in its filings changes. What does the voting/weighing framing say to do?
> > [!SUCCESS]- Answer
> > - **Short answer:** re-check value, not price; if the fundamentals are unchanged, the gap is opportunity, not information.
> > - **Why:** **Voting machine** ➔ sentiment moved the price; **weighing machine** ➔ long-run price converges on cash generation, which the news did not change.
