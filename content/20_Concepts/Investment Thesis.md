# [[Investment Thesis]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · the 5–6 bullets the whole report defends · built from [[Business Model Analysis]] + [[Competitive Positioning and Economic Moat]], priced by [[Target Price and Valuation Triangulation]]
**What it weighs:** what you believe that the price does not — and what would prove you wrong.

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Test question:** *what is the market not discounting, and what will make it re-price?*
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** forward-looking and comparative — each bullet says what **separates the company from competitors**, qualitatively **and** quantitatively; past performance alone is not a thesis.

## 📝 Core Commitments
- **Format** ➔ 5–6 key dot points summarising why you pitch the stock + the recommendation (Buy / Hold / Sell).
- **Content** ➔ the business's key advantages (qualitative + quantitative) · what separates it from competitors · forward-looking.
- **Purpose questions** ➔ what stands out? · what fuels it? · expected performance? · intrinsic value?
- **Mispricing + catalyst** ➔ explain *why* the security is mispriced and *what will prompt re-pricing* (CFA guide).
- **Falsifiability** ➔ state the belief the price doesn't reflect **and** the evidence that would prove you wrong (Fundamentals checklist Q6).
- **Every bullet maps to a line item** ➔ [[Equity Research Process (Story to Line Item)]].

## 🧩 Case Application Drill
> [!QUESTION]- Case 1: Draft the skeleton of a BUY thesis for Tenaga Nasional using the Monash Team B report's facts — current RM13.54, DCF target RM16.09 (+18.8%), >50% generation share, sole single buyer, regulated return 7.3% on a RAB growing at 5.7% CAGR, data-centre demand, NETR transition.
> > [!SUCCESS]- Model verdict
> > - **Call:** BUY, 12-month DCF target RM16.09, +18.8% upside.
> > - **Bullet — earnings visibility:** regulated monopoly ➔ allowed return on a growing RAB ➔ predictable cash flows (line item: RAB × 7.3%).
> > - **Bullet — growth driver:** structurally rising electricity demand from data centres ➔ sales GWh growth.
> > - **Bullet — capex as catalyst:** RAB expansion under NETR / contingent capex ➔ higher regulated earnings base.
> > - **Bullet — sustainability:** coal → renewables transition improves durability of regulated returns.
> > - **What would prove it wrong:** cut in allowed return (−100 bp ≈ −10% fair value of the regulated business), capex under-spend shrinking RAB, unrecovered fuel costs.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **Description posing as thesis** ➔ "TNB is Malaysia's largest utility" is a fact everyone has priced; the thesis is the *gap* vs consensus.
- 💡 **No falsifier** ➔ a thesis with no "what would change my mind" can't be monitored or defended in Q&A.
