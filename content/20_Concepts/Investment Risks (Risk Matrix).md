# [[Investment Risks (Risk Matrix)]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · "what could kill it?" · 15/100 report marks ([[CFA Institute Research Challenge (Rules and Scoring)]]) · quantified by [[Sensitivity and Scenario Analysis]]
**What it weighs:** which negative developments could break the thesis, how likely, how big, and what mitigates them.

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Test question:** for each risk — **impact** on the valuation, **probability**, and **mitigation** — plotted on a probability × impact matrix.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** quantify where possible (TNB: −100 bp allowed return ≈ −10% fair value of the regulated business); an unranked list of generic risks earns little.

## 📝 Core Commitments
- **Categories (CFA guide)** ➔ operational · financial · regulatory · legal; reports also group market risks and ESG risks.
- **Analyst's job** ➔ disclosures list risks, but risks are subjective and hard to quantify (e.g. a competing technology) ➔ *you* judge them.
- **Automatic red flags** ➔ auditor's **qualified opinion**; **material weakness** in internal control over financial reporting.
- **Format used by reports** ➔ risk code (M1, R2 …) → description → **Impact** → **Mitigation** → place on matrix.

## ⚖️ Framework Contrast — TNB risk register (Monash Team B)
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fuel price volatility & recovery lag | 6-month ICPT lag ballooned receivables ➔ working-capital strain, extra borrowing | Automatic Fuel Adjustment (AFA) from 1 Jul 2025: monthly tariff adjustments capped ±10% |

| Allowed-return cut at RP5 | −100 bp ≈ −10% regulated-business fair value | active government engagement; 7.3% kept for RP4 |
| Carbon pricing / ESG compliance | exclusion from ESG funds ➔ P/E compression | decarbonisation plan; MSCI ESG upgraded to A (2025) |

## 🧩 Case Application Drill
> [!QUESTION]- Case 1: Company A (cake & delights distributor, 85% COGS ratio, 98% of PPE is motor vehicles on finance leases, FY25 headcount fell 62 → 24). Write two risks in impact/mitigation form.
> > [!SUCCESS]- Model verdict
> > - **Input-cost / margin risk:** COGS ≈ 85% of revenue ➔ a 1-pt rise in COGS % cuts EBIT margin by 1 pt (~16% of FY25 EBIT) ➔ mitigation: pricing power / supplier terms — test with a COGS-% sensitivity.
> > - **Operational / key-staff risk:** sharp headcount cut + lease-funded fleet ➔ distribution capacity and service levels at risk ➔ mitigation: evidence of outsourcing or productivity gains; model capex for fleet renewal.
> > - **Form check:** each risk names the line item it hits and the size of the hit.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **Generic risks** ➔ "economic downturn" with no company-specific transmission to a line item.
- 💡 **No mitigation / no probability** ➔ the matrix needs both axes.
