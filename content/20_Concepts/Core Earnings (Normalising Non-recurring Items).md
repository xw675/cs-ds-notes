# [[Core Earnings (Normalising Non-recurring Items)]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · the earnings base you forecast and apply multiples to · CFA guide: "nonrecurring events … can distort a firm's financial results"

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Objective:** strip one-offs out of reported profit ➔ **Core EBITDA** and **Core PAT** = the repeatable earning power.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** add back **expenses** that won't recur and remove **gains** that won't recur — the sign of the adjustment follows the item.

## 📝 Core
- **EBITDA** ➔ $\text{EBITDA} = \text{EBIT (profit from operations)} + \text{D\&A}$.
- **Core lines (model)** ➔ $\text{Core EBITDA} = \text{EBITDA} + \text{NRI}$; $\text{Core PAT} = \text{PAT} + \text{NRI}$ where $\text{NRI} = -\sum(\text{one-off lines})$ (expenses stored negative ➔ add-back positive; other operating income ➔ deducted).
- **Items Company A treats as non-recurring** ➔ other operating income · audit fee under-provision (prior year) · condolence · donation & gift · entertainment · irrecoverable debts · loss on disposal of PPE · penalty.
- **Size check** ➔ FY24 NRI $= +31.3$ (Core PAT 885.6 vs reported 854.3, +3.7%); FY25 $+8.9$.
- **Used downstream** ➔ ROE, margins, and implied multiples ($\text{EV}/\text{Core EBITDA}$, $\text{Equity}/\text{Core PAT}$) all use core figures ➔ [[Ratios for Shortlisting vs Evaluating]].
- **Other distortions to read the notes for (CFA guide)** ➔ off-balance-sheet financing, income & reserve recognition, depreciation policies.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **Recurring "one-offs"** ➔ an item that shows up most years (restructuring every year) is a cost of doing business — don't keep adding it back.
- 💡 **Simplification in the tax line** ➔ the model adds NRI to PAT gross of tax; strictly the add-back should be after-tax where the item was tax-deductible *(not in the slides)*.

## 🧠 Active Recall
> [!FAQ]- Why value a company on Core PAT rather than reported PAT?
> > [!SUCCESS]- Answer
> > - **Short answer:** valuation capitalises *repeatable* earnings; one-offs don't repeat.
> > - **Why:** **Multiple × base** ➔ a one-off gain or loss in the base year is multiplied by the PE / EV-EBITDA (or compounded into a forecast), mis-pricing the stock by far more than the item itself.
