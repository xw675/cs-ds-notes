# [[ESG in Equity Research]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · 15/100 report marks, 10/100 presentation marks ([[CFA Institute Research Challenge (Rules and Scoring)]]) · Fundamentals rule: "ESG leader" means nothing until it lands on a line item
**What it weighs:** whether ESG factors change cash flows, risk (discount rate) or the multiple — or are already priced in.

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Test question:** *does this ESG factor move a margin, the discount rate, the growth period, or the multiple?* If yes, model it; if no, say why it's valuation-neutral.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** describing policies earns little; graders want the **link to value** (or an argued "no adjustment").

## 📝 Core Commitments
| Pillar | What to assess (CFA guide) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Environmental** — conserving the natural world | climate change & carbon emissions, air/water pollution, energy efficiency, waste management |
| **Social** — people & relationships | community relations, human rights, gender & diversity, labour standards, customer satisfaction, employee engagement |
| **Governance** — standards for running the company | board composition, audit committee, executive compensation, succession planning, leadership experience, bribery & corruption policies |

- **Transmission channels seen in reports** ➔ ESG-adjusted **beta** (DOM: Sustainalytics 0.95 vs 1.0 baseline) · **P/E compression** via exclusion from ESG funds (TNB risk R2) · **cheaper financing** via green bonds / SLLs (DOM opportunity, WACC down) · executive pay tied to ESG targets (governance).
- **Rating + rationale** ➔ TNB: proprietary ★★★ "valuation neutral"; external MSCI rating upgraded to A in 2025.

## 🧩 Case Application Drill
> [!QUESTION]- Case 1: DOM Development has a strong ESG scorecard and a Sustainalytics ESG-adjusted beta of 0.95. Should the team lower its WACC?
> > [!SUCCESS]- Model verdict
> > - **Framework applied:** adjust only if ESG demonstrably changes risk or financing cost in *this* market.
> > - **Evidence:** expert interviews said ESG has minimal influence on financing decisions in Polish residential real estate; no notable green-bond / SLL issuance in the sector.
> > - **Verdict:** **no adjustment** — ESG judged already priced in; strength shown on the scorecard instead. Name the trigger that would change this (sector green-financing take-up).

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **ESG as a brochure** ➔ listing initiatives without saying which line item (or why none) they affect.
- 💡 **Double counting** ➔ lowering beta *and* raising the multiple for the same ESG strength.
