# [[Industry Analysis (Headwinds and Tailwinds)]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · "do I understand what drives growth and protects profits across the industry?" · the company-level half is [[Competitive Positioning and Economic Moat]]

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Objective:** define the industry → size its tailwinds/headwinds → identify key players and how they fared under the same conditions.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** PESTLE / Five Forces / SWOT are **working tools, not your final output** — the report needs the *conclusion* they lead to.

## 📝 Core
### 1. Three building blocks
| Block | Questions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Industry definition** | which industry? what products/services define it? positioning — leader, market share, monopoly or oligopoly? |
| **Headwinds & tailwinds** | current market size (industry value) · attractive to enter — booming or matured? · which risks to prepare for / mitigate? |
| **Key players** | major companies in the same market · under the same circumstances, what happened to peers? · same risk/reward perceived? |

### 2. Tailwinds vs headwinds — market trends, natural advantages, structural changes
| Sector | Tailwinds | Headwinds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Technology / software | digitalisation, IT spending, AI integration, cloud adoption | data privacy, cybersecurity risk |
| Renewable energy | environmental concerns, government policy, transition to renewables | weather dependence, infrastructure limits |
| Healthcare / pharma | ageing population, disease prevalence, public awareness | pricing pressure, regulatory & approval risk |

### 3. Frameworks (tools, not output)
- **Macro factors (PESTLE-style)** ➔ political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental drivers.
- **Competitive forces (Porter's Five Forces)** ➔ "effective tool for examining the health and competitive intensity of an industry" (CFA guide).
- **SWOT** ➔ strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.
- **Also check** ➔ production capacity levels, pricing, distribution, stability of market share.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **Pasting the framework diagram** ➔ a filled-in SWOT is not analysis; state which force/trend changes the forecast and how.
- 💡 **Ignoring peer outcomes** ➔ "what happened to peers under the same conditions" is the cheapest evidence you have.
