# [[Ratios for Shortlisting vs Evaluating]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · the two jobs ratios do · evaluating side = [[ROIC vs Cost of Capital]], [[DuPont Decomposition (ROE)]], [[Free Cash Flow (Cash vs Profit)]]; shortlisting side feeds [[Relative Valuation (Trading Multiples)]]
**What it weighs:** whether a ratio is answering *"is it worth my 40 hours?"* (outside-in) or *"is it a good business, at what price?"* (inside-out).

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Test question:** does this ratio use the **market price**? Yes ➔ shortlisting (what the market expects). No ➔ evaluating (whether the business creates value).
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** **a low PE is a reason to look, never a reason to buy** — most beginner mistakes use a shortlisting ratio to answer an evaluating question.

## 📝 Core Commitments
| | Job one — Shortlisting | Job two — Evaluating |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Question | "Which of the 900+ Bursa companies deserve my next 40 hours?" | "Is this a good business, and at what price?" |
| Direction | **outside-in** — uses the market price | **inside-out** — ignores the market price |
| Ratios | market multiples: PE, EV/EBITDA, P/B, dividend yield | returns & cash: ROIC, DuPont, free cash flow |
| Cost | fast, cheap, screenable, shallow | slow, manual, requires reading the notes |
| Tells you | what the market **expects** | whether the business **creates or destroys value** |

- **Multiple in the model** ➔ the Company A workbook backs out implied multiples from the DCF: $\text{EV/EBITDA} = \dfrac{\text{EV}}{\text{Core EBITDA}}$, $\text{P/E} = \dfrac{\text{Equity value}}{\text{Core PAT}}$ ➔ see [[Core Earnings (Normalising Non-recurring Items)]].

## 🧩 Case Application Drill
> [!QUESTION]- Case 1: Press Metal (PMETAL) trades at ~24x trailing PE, A-Rank (ARANK) at ~7x. Both make aluminium products in Malaysia. A screen flags A-Rank as "three times cheaper". Verdict?
>
> | FY2025 (approx.) | Press Metal | A-Rank |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | Business | integrated smelter + extruder | secondary billet remelter |
> | Revenue | ~RM16.2bn | ~RM552m |
> | Net profit | ~RM2.1bn | ~RM10m |
> | Net margin | ~13% | ~1.9% |
> | Market cap | ~RM65bn | ~RM71m |
> | Trailing PE | ~24x | ~7x |
> | Other | ROE ~23%; 1.08 Mt smelting; first-quartile cost curve | gross margin ~4.2%; ~132k t capacity |
>
> > [!SUCCESS]- Model verdict
> > - **Framework applied:** PE is a shortlisting ratio ➔ it found a *difference*; only business analysis explains it.
> > - **Evaluation:** A-Rank buys scrap, remelts, sells billets ➔ metal price passes through, thin spread, little pricing power, small scale, fragile earnings ➔ low multiple is appropriate. Press Metal owns a structural cost advantage (long-term hydropower) competitors cannot replicate ➔ paid up.
> > - **Verdict:** **not three times cheaper — priced for what it is.** Two different businesses, two appropriate multiples.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
- 💡 **Screen = conclusion** ➔ stopping at "PE below peers" without the margin/moat/returns analysis behind the gap.
- 💡 **Mixing jobs** ➔ quoting a multiple as proof of quality; multiples encode expectations, not value creation.
