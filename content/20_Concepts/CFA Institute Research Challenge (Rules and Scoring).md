# [[CFA Institute Research Challenge (Rules and Scoring)]]

**Context:** [[MUIG_AAP_MOC]] · the competition the AAP report format targets (Lesson 1 opens with it) · section contents in [[Equity Research Report Structure]]

> [!abstract] Quick Revision
> - **🎯 Objective:** team of 3–5 researches one assigned listed company ➔ 10-page written report + 10-minute presentation + Q&A, scored on fixed sheets.
> - **⚠️ Key Constraint:** **Valuation and Financial Analysis carry 40 of 100 report marks**; Business Description only 5 ➔ allocate pages accordingly.

## 📝 Core
### 1. Written report — format rules
- **Length** ➔ ≤ 10 A4 pages (excl. CFA cover) **+** appendix ≤ 10 A4 pages.
- **First-page header** ➔ company name · exchange · ticker · sector/industry · recommendation (buy/sell/hold) · current price (as of date) · target price (% up/downside).
- **Required sections** ➔ business description · industry overview & competitive positioning · investment summary · valuation · financial analysis · investment risks · ESG (extra headings allowed).
- **Integrity** ➔ public information only · independent-analyst perspective · original work · cited (Chicago / Harvard / MLA) · CFA cover unaltered · AI use must be disclosed and documented.
- **Plagiarism includes** ➔ unattributed excerpts · quotes attributed to unnamed "leading analysts" · others' forecasts without their caveats · charts without sources · copied spreadsheets/algorithms.

### 2. Scoring sheets
| Report section | Max | | Presentation criterion | Max |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Business description | 5 | | Financial analysis (industry, company, competitors) | 20 |
| Industry overview & competitive positioning | 10 | | Valuation (methods appropriate & detailed) | 20 |
| Investment summary | 15 | | ESG | 10 |
| **Valuation** | **20** | | Presentation (logical; facts support the call) | 20 |
| **Financial analysis** | **20** | | Q&A | 20 |
| Investment risks | 15 | | Team involvement | 5 |
| ESG | 15 | | Materials (slide quality) | 5 |

- **Tiebreak order (report)** ➔ financial analysis → valuation → investment summary → industry → risks → ESG → business description.

### 3. Presentation & contact rules
- **Timing** ➔ 10-min presentation; Q&A 10 min (local, regional semifinal) or 15 min (regional & global finals); **no Q&A at sub-regional**.
- **Conduct** ➔ no props (cue cards OK) · must show sources · only team members present · only judges ask questions.
- **Company contact** ➔ one informational session + at most one follow-up; customers/suppliers/competitors may be contacted only identifying as students.

## 🧠 Active Recall
> [!FAQ]- You have one spare page in the report. Where do the marks say it should go, and why?
> > [!SUCCESS]- Answer
> > - **Short answer:** valuation or financial analysis.
> > - **Why:** **Mark weights** ➔ 20 each (and first in the tiebreak) vs 5 for business description; depth there also feeds the 20-point Q&A defence.
